# 🌸 Our Little World — Cozy Edition
**Two hearts. One world. Endless adventures. ♡**

Welcome to **Our Little World**, a cozy Minecraft Fabric experience designed for two players who love building, exploring, decorating, and creating beautiful memories together.

Whether you're building your dream cottage, discovering breathtaking landscapes, decorating your home, or growing your own little farm, every adventure becomes a shared story.

## ✨ Features

- 🏡 **Cozy Building & Decoration** — Create beautiful homes, bridges, and charming villages with additional decorative blocks and furniture.
- 🌿 **Exploration & Nature** — Discover beautiful landscapes, diverse biomes, and wildlife.
- 🥕 **Farming & Cooking** — Grow crops, prepare delicious meals, and build a peaceful countryside life.
- 🌷 **Aesthetic Atmosphere** — Enjoy enhanced visuals, textures, and animations.
- ⚡ **Optimized Performance** — A Fabric-based experience with performance-focused improvements.
- 💕 **Made for Two** — A relaxing survival adventure to enjoy together.

## 🎮 Gameplay

- **Minecraft:** Java Edition 26.3
- **Mod Loader:** Fabric
- **Game Mode:** Survival
- **Style:** Cozy, Adventure, Exploration, Building & Farming
